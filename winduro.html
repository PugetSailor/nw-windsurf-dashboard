Leg by leg

  • Start to the hi-rise (5–8 min, downwind). Fast. Mats (2022) passed a bit away from Mercer to gain on the boat hugging it.
  • Mercer's lee: up the East Channel and round the north tip (15–25 min). The big shadow. Watch for puffs off the Bellevue side, then the Mercer side; stay off the Luther Burbank shore. Don't burn energy pumping early.
  • North tip to the floating bridge (upwind). The wind comes back hard and gusty. Daggerboard down, rig forward, harness lines right. Make one long tack to the west opening rather than short-tacking the Mercer shore (Giovanni beat Mats that way in 2022).
  • Through the west opening. Short tacks, well clear of the piers: behind a pillar the wind vanishes. Get away from the chop and reflected waves on the windward (south) side quickly.
  • The beat to the south tip (64–86 min for 2025 longboards). Daggerboard fully down and point. Use the full width of the Slot between Seward Park and Mercer. Lay the south tip with one long tack instead of pinching.
  • Home (15–30 min). Run straight downwind to the dock. If tired, don't risk gybes: Greg lost 1.5 hours to crashes and rests in 2025.
  • Kit: phone in a waterproof bag clipped twice, water and snacks, warm suit for 45–55°F and rain. Sign out at the library box when you finish.
